    What these numbers are

    Values are estimates from AcousticBrainz, a volunteer collection of audio analyses that closed in 2022. Its replay-gain and dynamic-complexity values were calibrated against direct EBU R128 measurements of the same audio: about ±1.2 LU per track for loudness and ±3–4 LU for loudness range, which is a coarser measure.

    An album's value is the median of its tracks (at least 3) on its most-submitted original edition, using lossless copies; bonus tracks from deluxe editions don't count. Only original releases are included, not reissues or compilations of older recordings. Remasters often share recording IDs with the original, so a few albums may mix copies of different masters.

    "Louder than 80%" compares the album with other albums released within two years of it, across all genres or within its genre (from MusicBrainz tags). Data: AcousticBrainz and MusicBrainz, CC0.
